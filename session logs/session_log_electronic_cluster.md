## Theory

The electronic cluster is structured around a single genealogical spine: **Elektronische Musik → electronic → synthpop / ambient / electronica latina / IDM**, with two parallel lineages branching off from it.

The first is the **dance lineage**: funk → disco → hi-nrg → house, which generates house's subgenres (melodic house, gqom, uk garage, stutter house) and feeds eurodance through both disco and hi-nrg. This lineage lives in the soul-rnb-funk cluster but its children (house and everything below it) belong to electronic — disco is the bridge.

The second is the **atmospheric/beat lineage**: Elektronische Musik → ambient → downtempo → dreamy electronic / lo-fi / chillwave, plus hip hop → trip hop → downtempo as a parallel origin point. This lineage produces the slower, more textural nodes.

Around these two spines, several genres arrived from adjacent clusters — post-punk and new wave gave rise to ebm, coldwave, electroclash, electropop, and alternative dance. These nodes naturally float between the electronic and rock clusters because they have parents in both worlds. That scattering is structurally correct, not a bug.

Leaf nodes (gqom, eurodance, jungle, indie dance, cosmic disco, chillwave, lo-fi, electroclash, ambient) have one parent each and no children. They will always sit at the periphery. That is accurate musicology — they are genre endpoints, not hubs.
